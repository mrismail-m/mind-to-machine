A few things to know.

Can students from other universities participate?

Yes. Mind to Machine is a national-level hackathon bringing together student participants from universities across Pakistan. Detailed eligibility requirements will be shared before registration opens.

What will we be building?

You’ll work from a problem statement to develop a solution, then present it for evaluation. The challenge, submission requirements, and judging criteria will be announced by the organizers.

How many people can be on a team?

Participate solo or in a team of up to 3 members. Solo participants leave the additional team member fields empty.

When is it, and how should I plan my travel?

18 November 2026, at New Building, B26 New Hall, COMSATS University Islamabad, Wah Campus. Check-in runs from 9:00 to 10:00 AM. Transport and accommodation are not confirmed.

What are the prizes and swag?

The total cash prize pool is PKR 50,000. Every participant gets a sticker, pen, bag, complimentary lunch, and participation certificate.
