03 / Arrive ready

A little preparation goes a long way.

  • Bring your laptop, charger, and the development tools you plan to use.
  • Keep your student identification and payment reference available for the registration desk.
  • Arrive for check-in between 9:00 and 10:00 AM at New Building, B26 New Hall.
  • Solo participants are welcome. Teams may have no more than 3 members.

Campus entry requirements, internet availability, and any travel arrangements will be confirmed by the organizers.

04 / Build, submit, present

Problem statement to solution.

The challenge is revealed during the 10:00–10:30 AM opening. Building runs from 10:30 AM to 2:00 PM, followed by project submission and lunch from 2:00–2:30 PM. Jamming and evaluation run from 2:30–4:30 PM.

The problem statement, permitted tools, submission format, evaluation criteria, prize distribution, and detailed event rules will be shared by the organizing team. This handbook does not assign unconfirmed scoring weights or technology restrictions.

The three judges will be revealed soon.
